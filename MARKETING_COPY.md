### 3. Parent FAQ (Quick Airport Answers)

- **Q: Does this work without Wi-Fi in Airplane Mode?**
  *A: Yes! Sliver Kids v2 ULTRA is engineered as a zero-dependency Progressive Web Application (PWA) with fully offline WASM core computation.*

- **Q: Are there any ads, in-app purchases, or tracking?**
  *A: Absolutely zero. No ads, no popups, no external tracking, and no hidden subscriptions.*

- **Q: Can we print the outlines or save pictures?**
  *A: Yes! Use the "Print" button to generate clean black & white coloring sheets, or save finished masterpieces directly to the in-browser "My Saved Art" gallery.*
